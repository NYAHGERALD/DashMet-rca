# DASHMET OPERATIONS INTELLIGENCE
## Enterprise Operations Management for Food Safety, Manufacturing, and Team Execution
## USA • Mexico • Canada | Multi-Language | Enterprise Scale

---

## 1. SYSTEM PURPOSE & SCOPE

This system is a **multi-tenant, enterprise-grade AI-powered Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Web Application** designed for:

- Food Manufacturing
- Warehousing & Logistics
- General Manufacturing

The system supports:
- **Food Safety RCA**
- **Machine & Equipment RCA**
- **AI-Assisted Analysis**
- **5 Whys & Fishbone & Advanced Methods**
- **Multi-Site, Multi-Role, Multi-Language Operation**
- **Regulatory & Audit Compliance**

Target Regions:
- USA
- Mexico
- Canada

Primary Languages:
- English
- Spanish
- (Optional) French

## 2. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

- Multi-Tenant Architecture (each company isolated)

## 6. INCIDENT CAPTURE ENGINE

### 6.1 Incident Creation Flow
1. Select **Type**:
   - Food Safety
   - Machine & Equipment

2. Dynamic Category Dropdown (Type-Based Filtering)

3. Sub-Category Dropdown

4. If "Other" → Display Custom Title Input

5. Context Fields:
   - Facility
   - Line / Area
   - Product
   - Lot Number
   - Date & Time
   - Shift
   - Machine ID (if applicable)

6. Evidence Upload
   - Photos
   - Videos
   - Files
   - Voice Notes (Auto Transcribed)

7. AI Summary Generator
   - Converts raw text into professional incident description
