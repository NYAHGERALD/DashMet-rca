# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 12, 2026
**Product:** DashMet Operations Intelligence / DashMet RCA Engine
**Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how **404UPDATE**, operating DashMet Operations Intelligence and DashMet RCA Engine ("DashMet", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects information when authorized users access or use DashMet’s web application, mobile application, APIs, notifications, reporting tools, collaboration tools, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

DashMet is a private enterprise operations, safety, food safety, root cause analysis, CAPA, bakery metrics, meeting, and compliance platform. The Services are intended for authorized organizations and invited users, not for general public social networking or consumer advertising.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Terms of Service and Cookie Policy.

## 1. Roles: Organization Data and User Data

DashMet is used by organizations to manage their own operational records. In many cases, the organization that provides you access to DashMet controls the records, users, permissions, retention settings, and business purposes for the data stored in its DashMet workspace.

For organization-controlled records, DashMet generally acts as a service provider or processor that handles data on behalf of the organization. Your organization may decide:

- Who can use DashMet.
- What role and permissions each user has.
- Which incidents, RCA records, CAPA records, bakery metrics, workplace safety records, meeting records, and support records are created.
- How records are reviewed, exported, retained, restricted, or deleted.
- Whether certain features, notifications, recordings, or integrations are enabled.

DashMet may also process some information for its own business and security purposes, such as account security, service reliability, support, fraud prevention, billing, legal compliance, and system administration.

If you have questions about organization-controlled data, contact your organization administrator first. If you have questions about DashMet’s privacy practices, contact us using the information in Section 18.

## 2. Information We Collect

The information DashMet collects depends on the features your organization enables and how users interact with the Services.

### 2.1 Account and Profile Information

We may collect:

- Name.
- Email address.
- Phone number, if provided or used for verification or notifications.
- Job role, DashMet role, privileges, and organization membership.
- Organization, facility, department, area, line, shift, and team associations.
- Language, timezone, notification preferences, and interface preferences.
- Authentication and account status information.

### 2.2 Authentication, Security, and Device Information

We may collect:

- Login events, logout events, session events, password reset events, and verification events.
- IP address, browser type, user-agent, device information, and timestamps.
- Trusted-device records when a user chooses "Remember this device".
- Account lockout, failed login, rate limit, and security audit events.
- Session identifiers, refresh records, and token hashes used to protect the Services.
- Mobile secure-storage identifiers needed to maintain a saved mobile session or trusted-device flow.

DashMet does not receive or store biometric templates. If a user enables Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock in the mobile app, biometric matching is handled by the user’s device operating system.

### 2.3 Incident, RCA, CAPA, and Compliance Records

Users and organizations may submit information such as:

- Incident number, type, category, severity, status, date, time, location, facility, department, area, line, shift, product name, lot number, and equipment information.
- Incident descriptions, AI summaries, root cause analysis, fishbone analysis, corrective actions, preventive controls, CAPA board items, owners, due dates, progress, completion notes, verification notes, and regulatory tags.
- Food safety and foreign material details, including product, hold, disposition, contamination window, source analysis, prevention measures, review notes, and supporting evidence.
- Workplace safety details, including employee name, job information, contact information, injury details, body parts affected, medical-treatment indicators, witness names, supervisor actions, OSHA-related fields, and investigation findings when entered by authorized users.
- Support records, issue reports, machine issues, quality issues, operational notes, and audit trail information.

Some of this information may include personal information or sensitive personal information if entered by users or required by an organization’s workflow.

### 2.4 Evidence, Files, Photos, Videos, Audio, and Recordings

Users may upload or create:

- Photos and videos.
- Documents and forms.
- Voice notes and audio recordings.
- Team meeting recordings.
- Transcripts, AI-generated meeting summaries, and discussion markers.
- Evidence annotations, file names, file types, file sizes, storage paths, upload timestamps, and uploader information.

Users and organizations are responsible for ensuring they have the right to collect, record, upload, and process this content.

### 2.5 Team Collaboration and Communications

DashMet may collect and store:

- Incident chat messages.
- Discussion posts, comments, replies, reactions, announcements, mentions, and status updates.
- Team participant records and invitation status.
- Action assignments, handoffs, decision notes, question records, and update records.

Private messaging and collaboration content may be visible to authorized users and administrators according to organization permissions.

### 2.6 Bakery Metrics and Operations Data

DashMet may collect bakery and production operations information, including:

### 2.8 Usage, Diagnostics, and Reliability Information

We may collect:

- Pages, screens, features, and workflows used.
